1. Executive Summary
NOLT Finance currently uses multiple HR forms, policy acknowledgements, document uploads and manual
review steps during the employee lifecycle. This PRD defines a secure web-based portal that centralises those
activities, gives employees a clear checklist and progress view, and gives HR a controlled review and audit
workflow.
Product vision
A single HR lifecycle portal where HR can invite an employee, assign the correct requirements, collect forms and
supporting documents, review each item, request corrections with reasons, complete induction steps, track company
assets, and later manage exit clearance without relying on fragmented email chains.
1.1 Core outcomes
 Reduce HR follow-up effort by making requirements, due items and rejection reasons visible to employees.
 Create one source of truth for onboarding status, submissions, approvals, acknowledgements and audit
history.
 Convert suitable paper/PDF forms into structured digital forms while retaining upload capability where
originals or supporting evidence are required.
 Provide role-based access to sensitive personal, health, banking and employment information.
 Make the platform extensible from onboarding into a complete employee lifecycle process, including
offboarding and asset return.
1.2 Recommended delivery approach

3. Product Context, Problem and Goals
3.1 Current-state problems
 Employees can be unsure which forms and documents are still outstanding.
 HR must manually chase submissions and explain rejected items by email or messaging.
 Information is duplicated across forms and spreadsheets, increasing re-entry and inconsistency.
 Approvals are difficult to audit when they happen outside a central system.
 Sensitive documents may be shared through channels that do not provide appropriate access control or
retention management.
 Company asset records used during onboarding are not automatically connected to offboarding return
checks.
3.2 Product goals
 Provide a secure employee self-service onboarding workspace.
 Enable HR to assign requirements by employee type or onboarding template.
 Provide item-level statuses and reasons for required corrections.
 Allow forms to be saved as drafts and resumed later.
 Maintain versioned acknowledgements for policies and agreements.
 Support document review, resubmission and audit history.
 Enable structured export where third-party providers require spreadsheets or documents.
 Create a foundation for employee exit clearance and asset return.
3.3 Non-goals for the first release
 Payroll processing
 Full performance management
 Leave management
 Recruitment applicant tracking before offer acceptance
 Employee scheduling/time-and-attendance
 Replacing third-party HMO/insurance systems themselves

 Health and bank/pension/tax information should be separated from general onboarding permissions.
 Employee-submitted files must be private by default; no public URLs.
 Every approval, rejection, resubmission, policy acknowledgement and administrative change must be
attributable to a user and timestamp.

7. Functional Requirements - NOLT-Specific Modules
7.1 Employee Data / Biodata
 Capture name, employment date/type, date/place of birth, nationality, marital details, residence/address,
contact information, state of origin/LGA and emergency contact.
 Capture education history with qualification and date.
 Capture bank name/account number, pension administrator/number and tax ID.
 Capture next of kin details.
 Capture health information in a separately permissioned subsection.
 Where HR already entered employee identity/employment data, fields should be prefilled to reduce
duplicate entry.
7.2 Background Declaration
 Yes/No questions for criminal record, fraudulent/dishonest/misconduct activity, and investigations.
 If any answer is Yes, explanation becomes mandatory.
 Employee must confirm truthfulness and consent to verification/background checks before submission.
 Declaration version, timestamp and employee acknowledgement must be retained.
7.3 Supporting Documents
 Configurable upload slots for passport photo, pre-employment medical report, former employer reference,
character reference, birth certificate, secondary certificate, degree certificate, NYSC certificate/exemption,
postgraduate certificate and other HR-requested evidence.
 Allowed file types and maximum size must be configurable.
 HR can mark a document as Not Applicable or Waived with a reason.
 Each document has its own review status; one rejected document must not invalidate unrelated approved
items.
7.4 Guarantors and References
 Default template should support two guarantors and two references where required by HR policy.
 Employee enters guarantor/reference name, email and phone and sends a secure request.
 Guarantor can complete their own undertaking/details and upload supporting evidence without seeing the
employee portal.
 Guarantor evidence can include government ID, proof of address, proof of income and passport photograph
where required.
 HR can review each guarantor independently and request replacement/correction.
 Guarantor invitation status: Not Invited, Invited, Opened, Draft, Submitted, Changes Required, Approved.
HR decision required - conflicting source rule
The HR Policy Manual states an immediate-family member may be a guarantor, while the supplied Guarantor Form
states that a guarantor must not be a family member or relative. Production validation must remain configurable
until HR confirms the governing rule.

7.5 HR Policy Manual & Acknowledgement
 HR can upload/publish a policy manual version with effective date and version number.
 Employee can open/read the manual from the portal.
 Acknowledgement records staff name, date of resumption, date read, version read and
signature/acknowledgement date.
 If a new version is published, HR can require affected employees to acknowledge the new version without
overwriting earlier evidence.
7.6 Confidentiality Agreement
 Display the approved agreement text/version to the employee.
 Capture employee name, designation, signature/acknowledgement and date.
 Support NOLT signatory and witness sections where the approved legal process requires them.
 Retain an immutable completed copy or rendered agreement record for audit.
7.7 Employee Indemnity Agreement
 Populate employee identity/designation into the agreement where possible.
 Support staged execution by Employee -> Guarantor/Surety -> NOLT signatory as configured.
 Each signatory step is timestamped and cannot be silently overwritten.
 Completed agreement is locked and retained as a versioned record.
7.8 Beneficiary Nomination
 Capture employee/assured details.
 Allow one or more beneficiaries with surname, first name, relationship, allocation percentage, date of birth
and contact/address.
 Validate that total beneficiary percentage equals 100% before final submission, unless HR/provider rules
explicitly allow otherwise.
 Where a beneficiary is under 18, prompt for guardian/trustee details.
 Capture employee authorisation/signature and date.
 HR can export or download the completed record for insurance administration.
7.9 AXA HMO Enrollment
 Present the supplied AXA consent wording or an HR-approved current version and capture consent.
 Capture enrollee number (for additions), surname, first/middle name, plan, plan type, staff ID,
member/dependent status, relationship, enrollment date, sex, DOB, phone, pre-existing condition,
primary/secondary hospital, official email and address.
 Allow family/dependent rows where applicable.
 Provide an HR export matching the AXA enrollment spreadsheet columns so HR does not have to rekey
portal data manually.

10. Key User Stories and Acceptance Criteria
US-01 - As HR, I want to invite a new employee so they can complete onboarding before resumption.
 Acceptance: HR can create the employee and select an onboarding template.
 Acceptance: The system sends an invitation to the entered email.
 Acceptance: The invitation action is logged.
 Acceptance: HR can resend an invitation without creating a duplicate employee.
US-02 - As an employee, I want to see exactly what I need to complete so I do not have to ask HR.
 Acceptance: Dashboard lists every assigned requirement and its status.
 Acceptance: Outstanding/action-required items appear prominently.
 Acceptance: Progress updates after valid completion/approval events.
 Acceptance: Rejected items show the HR reason and a resubmit action.
US-03 - As HR, I want to reject an incomplete document with a reason so the employee knows what to fix.
 Acceptance: Reviewer cannot select Changes Required without entering a reason.
 Acceptance: Employee receives notification.
 Acceptance: Reason is visible on the relevant item only.
 Acceptance: Previous submission remains retained in history after replacement.
US-04 - As an employee, I want to save a long form and return later so I do not lose work.
 Acceptance: Save draft is available before final submission.
 Acceptance: Draft data is restored on next login.
 Acceptance: Incomplete required fields do not block saving a draft.
 Acceptance: Final submission does enforce required validation.
US-05 - As HR, I want guarantors to submit information directly so sensitive supporting evidence is not passed
through the employee unnecessarily.
 Acceptance: Employee/HR can trigger a secure guarantor invitation.
 Acceptance: Guarantor sees only their request.
 Acceptance: Link expires or requires verification.
 Acceptance: HR can review the guarantor response independently.
US-06 - As HR, I want policy acknowledgements tied to versions so I can prove what an employee accepted.
 Acceptance: Each acknowledgement references policy version/effective date.
 Acceptance: Employee identity and timestamp are retained.
 Acceptance: Publishing a new version does not modify historical acknowledgement records.

12. Business Rules and Validation
Rule Definition
BR-01 An employee cannot see another employee record.
BR-02 A requirement can be mandatory, optional, not applicable or waived. Waivers require reason and authorised actor.
BR-03 An approved submission cannot be directly edited. A correction creates a new version/review cycle.
BR-04 Changes Required always requires reviewer comments/reason.
BR-05 Progress is calculated from assigned mandatory requirements, not from a fixed global list.
BR-06
Employee onboarding cannot be marked complete while
mandatory requirements remain unapproved/incomplete
unless explicitly waived.
BR-07 Beneficiary allocation should equal 100% before final submission unless HR/provider config says otherwise.
BR-08 Health and financial details require restricted access groups.
BR-09 External request links are scoped to one request and expire.
BR-10 Policy/agreement acknowledgements retain the exact version accepted.
BR-11 Assigned assets cannot be marked fully cleared at exit until each expected return is recorded or exception approved.
BR-12 Guarantor-family eligibility rule remains configurable pending HR decision.

14. Security, Privacy and Compliance Requirements
 All portal traffic must use HTTPS/TLS.
 Files must be stored privately with access controlled by authenticated, authorised requests.
 Passwords, if used, must be securely hashed; no plaintext password storage.
 Multi-factor authentication should be available for HR/Admin accounts and considered mandatory for
privileged access.
 Role-based access control must distinguish general HR data from sensitive health and financial information.
 Administrative and review actions must be logged.
 Uploaded files must be validated for permitted type/size and scanned for malicious content where
production infrastructure allows.
 Data retention/deletion rules must be defined by NOLT HR/legal/compliance before go-live.
 The platform should provide a process for correcting inaccurate personal data while preserving audit
integrity.
 Consent/acknowledgement evidence must include version and timestamp.
 Backups and recovery procedures must be defined for production.
Compliance note
The supplied agreements reference Nigerian data protection obligations. Before production launch, NOLT should
have legal/compliance review the portal privacy notice, consent wording, retention schedule and electronic-
signature approach. This PRD is a product specification, not legal advice.

17. Recommended Technical Architecture
The final technology stack can be selected by the development team. The product architecture should separate
the user interface, API/business logic, relational data, secure object/file storage, email service and
audit/notification components.
Layer Recommendation / responsibility
Frontend Responsive web application for HR, employee and external request flows.
Backend/API Authentication, permissions, lifecycle rules, review workflow, form validation, notifications and exports.
Relational database Employees, cases, requirements, statuses, submissions, reviews, acknowledgements, assets and audit metadata.
Private file storage Uploaded documents and generated signed/completed artefacts; access via short-lived authorised links.
Email provider Invitation, reminder and status notifications.
Background jobs Email sending, file scanning, export generation and scheduled reminders.
Observability Application logs, error tracking, health monitoring and audit monitoring.
A suitable implementation could use a modern web framework with PostgreSQL and private object storage. The
exact stack is intentionally not mandated by this PRD so the team can select tools that fit NOLT hosting, security
and maintenance requirements.

22. Definition of Done for the Product
The NOLT HR Employee Lifecycle Portal is considered successful when HR can move a new employee from
invitation through approved pre-onboarding and completed induction without relying on separate email threads
or manually maintained status spreadsheets, while the employee can always see what is required, what has
been approved, what needs correction and why.
MVP definition of done
HR creates employee -> system sends secure invite -> employee completes forms/uploads -> employee sees progress
-> HR reviews each requirement -> employee receives approval or correction reason -> employee resubmits where
required -> HR can confirm Ready to Resume and later complete induction -> every material action is recorded in
the audit history.
22.1 Suggested project name
NOLT HR Employee Lifecycle Portal (working name). The product can be renamed internally before UI design
and deployment.

 Final data dictionary and form schema
